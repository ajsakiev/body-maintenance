# Kettlebell Sessions

Week order: **Pace · Heavy · Push**

---

## Pace

**Warm-up — 8 min**

1. 2 min easy movement
2. 3–4 mobility moves (hips, upper back)
3. Bell ramp, working bell:
   - 10 halos
   - 5 goblet squats, 3-sec pause
   - 5 slow deadlifts
   - 2 × 10 swings, building
   - 3 cleans per side
   - 3 presses per side

**Session — 20-min AMRAP · nose breathing · 6–7/10 · working bell**

| # | Movement | Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two-hand swing | 10 |
| 2 | Goblet squat | 5 |
| 3 | Push-up | 5 |
| 4 | Bent-over row | 5 per side |

**Cool-down — 5 min:** walk until HR is under 110 · hip-flexor stretch per side · dead hang

---

## Heavy

**Warm-up — 8 min**

1. 2 min easy movement
2. 3–4 mobility moves (hips, upper back)
3. 1 slow Turkish get-up per side
4. Bell ramp, working bell:
   - 10 halos
   - 5 goblet squats, 3-sec pause
   - 5 slow deadlifts
   - 2 × 10 swings, building
   - 3 cleans per side
   - 3 presses per side

**Session — 20-min cap · rest as needed between rounds · heavy bell**

| # | Movement | Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clean & strict press | 3 per side |
| 2 | Front squat, rack position | 5 per side |
| 3 | Kettlebell deadlift | 5 |
| 4 | Suitcase carry | 20 m per side |

**Cool-down — 5 min:** walk until HR is under 110 · hip-flexor stretch per side · dead hang

---

## Push

**Warm-up — 8 min**

1. 2 min easy movement
2. 3–4 mobility moves (hips, upper back)
3. Bell ramp, working bell:
   - 10 halos
   - 5 goblet squats, 3-sec pause
   - 5 slow deadlifts
   - 2 × 10 swings, building
   - 3 cleans per side
   - 3 presses per side

**Session — 20-min AMRAP · 9/10 (8/10 in weeks 1–2) · working bell**

| # | Movement | Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-arm swing *(or 16 two-hand)* | 8 per side |
| 2 | Goblet reverse lunge | 5 per side |
| 3 | Push press | 5 per side |
| 4 | One-arm row | 5 per side |

**Cool-down — 5 min:** walk until HR is under 110 · hip-flexor stretch per side · dead hang
