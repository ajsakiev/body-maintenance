# Body Maintenance: Evidence-Based Full Body Care

This guide is a living reference for building and maintaining a lean, flexible, mobile, long-lived body. Every recommendation is grounded in peer-reviewed research and rated by evidence strength.

---

## How to Use This Guide

Navigate the sections on the left. Each article covers:

1. **Why it matters** — the biological mechanism or longevity connection
2. **Core principles** — what the evidence actually says, concisely
3. **Practical protocols** — specific, actionable prescriptions you can follow
4. **Sources** — links to key studies, meta-analyses, and trusted researchers

---

## Evidence Rating System

Every major claim carries a badge:

**[STRONG]** Supported by multiple RCTs or systematic meta-analyses. High confidence.

**[MODERATE]** Supported by observational studies, smaller RCTs, or consistent expert consensus. Good confidence; some uncertainty remains.

**[EMERGING]** Promising early data from animal models, small human studies, or mechanistic research. Not yet consensus — worth watching.

## The Core Framework

This guide is organized around four interconnected goals:

| Goal | Why It Matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Lean body composition** | Excess adiposity drives inflammation, metabolic disease, and accelerated aging |
| **Functional flexibility & mobility** | Joint range of motion declines with age and disuse; reclaiming it reduces injury and improves quality of life |
| **Cardiovascular fitness** | VO₂ max is one of the strongest predictors of all-cause mortality ever identified |
| **Longevity & resilience** | Muscle mass, metabolic health, sleep quality, and diet quality together predict health span more than any single variable |

> **The Centenarian Decathlon model** (Peter Attia): Rather than training for today, train for the physical capacity you want at 80–90. What does your 85-year-old self need to be able to do? Work backwards from that goal.

## Where to Start

If you're new to intentional training:

1. **Mobility & Flexibility** — the most underpracticed and highest-ROI area for most people
2. **Cardiovascular** — Zone 2 training is low-effort and compounding over time
3. **Diet: Longevity & Anti-Inflammatory** — foundational nutrition patterns with the strongest evidence base

If you're already training consistently, the sections on **Recovery & Sleep** and **Gut & Metabolic Health** tend to surface the most overlooked improvements.
